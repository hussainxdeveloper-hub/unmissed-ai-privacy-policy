Privacy Policy

Unmissed AI · Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Unmissed AI app for Android (the “App”) handles information. The App is provided by Hussain (“we”, “us”, “the developer”). Please read it together with the policies of the services named below, because they apply to the parts of the App they run.

The short version
  • Your calls (reminders), voice notes, photos and settings are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them.
  • There are no accounts. You do not sign up or log in.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects limited technical data to show and measure ads. You can make choices about this (see Section 5).
  • Optional purchases are handled by Google Play and managed with RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the App keeps the following information in its private storage on your phone:

  • Calls you create: titles, messages, dates and times, repeat settings, labels, caller names and the sound you chose.
  • Voice notes you record or import for a call.
  • Photos you choose as a caller picture. The App saves a cropped copy of the part you select.
  • Your settings (for example sound, snooze, Quiet Hours) and a history of how your calls rang.
  • Calls you moved to the bin. They stay there for 30 days and are then deleted automatically, unless you restore or delete them sooner.
  • An unfinished draft, if you leave the create screen before saving.
  • Results of the App's setup and health checks (for example whether a permission is allowed).

This information stays on your device. The App does not send it to us or to any server of ours. Uninstalling the App removes the data it stores on your phone.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, call log, messages or GPS location.
  • The App's “calls” are reminders that look like an incoming call. The App does not make or receive real phone calls.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking tools in the App.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Services that collect information

The App uses the third-party services below. They run on your phone and send information to their own servers, so they are not covered by “stays on your device” above.

Google AdMob (ads)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use, for example: your advertising ID, your IP address (which can show an approximate location), device and app information (such as device model, Android version and app version), and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, Google asks for your consent first (see Section 5). More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing and RevenueCat (purchases)

If you buy something in the App, the payment is processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage purchases and check what you have unlocked. For this, RevenueCat receives, for example: an anonymous user ID that RevenueCat creates for the App, your purchase and subscription information from Google Play, and technical data such as your IP address, country, app version and Android version. RevenueCat does not know who you are, because the App has no accounts. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Google Play (crash and performance reports)

If you have turned on usage and diagnostics sharing on your phone, Google Play may give us anonymous crash and performance reports through the Google Play Console. This helps us fix problems. The App does not add its own crash-reporting service.

4. How we use information

  • Information on your phone is used only to run the App: to ring your calls on time, read them out loud, show your history and restore your data after a restart.
  • Information handled by AdMob is used to show ads and keep the App free or low cost.
  • Information handled by RevenueCat and Google Play is used to complete purchases and unlock what you bought.

5. Ads and your choices

  • Where ads appear: a short video you choose to watch before saving a new call, banner ads on some screens (such as Health and Bin), and a full-screen ad at some moments. The App does not show ads on the incoming call screen or while a call is ringing.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in one of these regions, the App shows Google's consent message the first time you open it. You can accept, manage or refuse. You can review or change your choice later using the privacy options in the App's Settings.
  • Personalized ads: on Android you can turn off ad personalization and delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings (usually under Privacy or Google, then Ads). You can also manage Google ad settings at My Ad Center. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

7. Features that use your phone's own services

  • Text-to-speech: the App uses your phone's built-in engine to read calls aloud. The engine and its provider depend on your phone, and the provider's own privacy policy may apply.
  • Ringtones: the App reads the list and name of your phone's built-in ringtones so you can choose and preview one. This stays on your phone. A choice made in the App does not change your phone's own ringtone.
  • Files and photos: you pick photos and audio files with your phone's file picker. The App copies the file you choose into its private storage. It does not browse your gallery or storage.
  • Sharing into the App: if you share a voice message from another app to Unmissed AI, the App saves a copy for the call you are creating.

8. Smart Quick Add (planned for a later update)

We plan to add Smart Quick Add, which lets you type a sentence such as “call mom tomorrow at 5pm” and fills in the time. When it is released, the text you type will be processed on your device using Google ML Kit. Before it first works, the App will ask your permission to download a small language file from Google's servers, and Google may receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) as with any download. We will update this policy before the feature is released.

10. Children

The App is a general reminder tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given personal information through the App, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

11. Your rights and choices

Because your calls stay on your phone, you are in control of them. You can edit or delete any call inside the App, and you can remove everything by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or some US states), you may have rights over personal information held about you, such as the right to access it, correct it, delete it, object to its use, or withdraw consent you gave. For information held by Google AdMob, use Google's own tools and settings in Section 5. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help you make the request. We do not hold other personal information about you.

12. How long we keep information

  • On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the App. Calls in the bin are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Advertising and purchase data held by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their own policies.

13. International data transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. They describe the safeguards they use in their own privacy policies.

14. Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage on your device. Your phone's own security (screen lock, encryption and updates) also helps protect it. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App grows, for example when we add new features or services. We will update it before releasing anything that changes how information is handled. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
