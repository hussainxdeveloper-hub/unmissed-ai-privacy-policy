Privacy Policy

Unmissed AI · Effective date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Unmissed AI app for Android (the “App”) handles information. The App is provided by Hussain (“we”, “us”, “the developer”).

The short version
  • Your reminders, voice notes, photos and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • There are no accounts, no cloud storage and no ads in the App.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal information.
  • The App needs the internet only for one optional download (see “Smart Quick Add” below).

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the App keeps the following information in its private storage on your phone:

  • Reminders you create: titles, descriptions, dates and times, repeat settings, labels and caller names.
  • Voice notes you record for a reminder.
  • Photos or avatars you choose for a caller.
  • Your settings (for example sound, snooze, Quiet Hours) and a history of how your reminders rang.
  • Results of the App's setup and health checks (for example whether a permission is allowed).

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it, and the App does not send it to us or to any server. Uninstalling the App removes the data it stores on your phone.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tools in the App, and we do not track you.
  • We do not sell your information.

3. Permissions the App asks for and why

Exact alarmsTo ring your reminders at the exact time you choose.
Full-screen alerts and showing over the lock screenTo display the incoming-call-style reminder screen when your phone is locked.

You can change or withdraw these permissions at any time in your phone's settings. Some features may stop working if you do.

4. Smart Quick Add (optional)

Smart Quick Add lets you type a sentence such as “call mom tomorrow at 5pm” and fills in the reminder time. The text you type is processed on your device using Google ML Kit. Before it first works, the App may ask your permission to download a small language file. That download is made from Google's servers, and Google may receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) as with any download. The text you type is not sent to us or to Google as part of this feature. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Reading reminders out loud

The App uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine to read reminders aloud. Which engine is used depends on your phone, and its provider's own privacy policy may apply to it.

6. Backups you create

If you use the backup feature, the App creates a file and saves or shares it where you choose. We do not receive this file. You are responsible for keeping it safe. Anyone who has the file can read the reminders inside it.

7. Crash and performance reports

If you have turned on usage and diagnostics sharing on your phone, Google Play may give us anonymous crash and performance reports through the Google Play Console. This helps us fix problems. The App does not add its own crash-reporting service.

8. Children

The App is a general reminder tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because the App does not collect personal information, there is none to delete.

9. Your rights and choices

Because your data stays on your phone, you are in control of it. You can edit or delete any reminder inside the App, and you can remove everything by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. If you have a question about your privacy or want to talk about your data, contact us using the details below.

10. Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage on your device. Your phone's own security (screen lock, encryption and updates) also helps protect it. No method of storing data is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App grows, for example if we add new features or services. If we add anything that changes how information is handled, we will update this policy before releasing it. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
